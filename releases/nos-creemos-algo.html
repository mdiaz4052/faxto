story

“Nos Creemos Algo” is FaXto’s first Spanish-language release on streaming platforms. Blending hyperpop and reggaetón, the song plays with the idea that we convince ourselves we are something while, in the cosmic sense, being nothing. It reduces us back to something more basic — animals making noise — while still insisting on the peculiar human habit of gathering the pieces, improvising rituals, and moving forward through life’s adversities.
